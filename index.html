🏡 Family Quest

A private chore tracker for one family. It isn't a public product, and it isn't available to download.

What it does

Kids tick off chores on a family tablet or phone and earn points, streaks and allowance. Parents approve chores and pay out. Everything runs on a small server in the family's home and is only reachable on the home Wi-Fi.

Google Tasks

The adults in the family can link their own Google account, so chores they choose appear as tasks in their Google Calendar. Ticking a task off there marks the chore as done in Family Quest. Family Quest only works with the one task list it creates, called "Family Quest".
